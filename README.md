# Alors on Danse AI Video

Put two people, two pets, or one of each into the Alors on danse keyboard meme.

**[Visit the website →](https://alorsondanseaivideo.net/)**

## What it does

Upload two separate photos: one for the keyboard player on the left, and one for the partner on the right. The website turns them into an AI remake of the Stromae and Jamel Debbouze white-studio sketch.

The scene, movement reference, and audio are already set. You choose who plays each role; there is no prompt to write or app to install.

## How to make a video

1. Open [alorsondanseaivideo.net](https://alorsondanseaivideo.net/).
2. Sign in with Google and choose a video pack.
3. Upload one photo for each role.
4. Generate the video, then download the finished MP4 from My videos.

Standard output is a 16-second, 720p, 16:9 landscape video with the original audio. Video packs are one-time purchases without a subscription.

Clear photos work best. AI can change facial details, anatomy, and motion, so results vary.
